## Summary

Tokens are rebuilt (with a fresh TinySegmenter and new regexes) on every render, and color indexing uses nested loops.

## Evidence

`new TinySegmenter()` inside buildTokensFromSegmenter called per item per render; inner `for j<i` index walks; per-word group scans.

## Proposed fix

Build tokens once per item, hoist the segmenter/regexes, and precompute prefix sums / word->group maps.

## Acceptance criteria

Re-rendering a full set does no re-tokenization and no nested full scans; visible render is smooth.

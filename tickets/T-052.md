## Summary

Absolutely positioned faces with a fixed min-height clip overflowing translated/longer text.

## Evidence

`.face { position:absolute; min-height:280px }`.

## Proposed fix

Stack faces with grid or allow overflow:auto and size by content.

## Acceptance criteria

Longer sentences remain fully visible in both list and practice views.

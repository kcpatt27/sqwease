## Summary

Hepburn tables, particle lists, and the JA SOV <-> EN SVO color-mapping are hardcoded into shared modules, so they cannot describe Spanish/French/Mandarin/Korean.

## Evidence

English grammar encoded in code; reversal formula assumes a JA->EN word-order change.

## Proposed fix

Move language alignment metadata behind a per-language strategy/config; keep the Japanese strategy as the default implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

Adding a non-Japanese target requires config/data, not edits to the generic coloring core.

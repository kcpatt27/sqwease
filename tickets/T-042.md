## Summary

Destructive reset uses a native confirm() that is unstyled, untranslatable, and blocks.

## Evidence

`if (!confirm('Reset all word lists to defaults? ...'))`.

## Proposed fix

Use an inline confirmation or a `<dialog>` with focus management.

## Acceptance criteria

Reset confirmation is keyboard-operable, labeled, and translatable.

## Summary

Every add/remove regenerates the entire dataset and re-renders all views.

## Evidence

saveWords -> generateItems + renderList + updatePracticeList + renderPracticeCard.

## Proposed fix

Update only the affected slot/set.

## Acceptance criteria

Adding one word updates only that category and the active set.

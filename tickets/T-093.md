## Summary

i18n `apply()` sets `document.title` to the plain `data-i18n` key value, so `<title>… · SQWease</title>` becomes just "Flashcards"/"1-2-3 Boxes".

## Evidence

<title data-i18n="nav.flashcards">Flashcards · SQWease</title> → title "Flashcards".

## Proposed fix

Add dedicated `title.*` keys, or append `app.name` in `apply()`.

## Acceptance criteria

Every page title includes the site name and remains localized.

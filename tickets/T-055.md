## Summary

178KB + 26KB + 25KB data modules are parsed/executed synchronously before content is interactive.

## Evidence

flashcards-vocab.js is 178KB on one line; loaded as a classic script at end of body.

## Proposed fix

Add `defer` to scripts, initialize data on demand (for example when Study Now is pressed), and avoid duplicate work; do not switch to fetch (would break file:// usage).

## Acceptance criteria

First paint/interactivity no longer waits on the full data parse where avoidable; file:// still works.

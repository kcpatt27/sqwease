## Summary

The Korean deck got ~75% of glosses from an unofficial Google Translate endpoint (ToS/privacy risk) and the Spanish deck's glosses/POS were authored by MT, both cross-checked against Wiktionary. Replace with a licensed bilingual source.

## Evidence

Korean agent report: 1,770/2,500 via "Google Translate (unofficial endpoint)"; Spanish agent report: glosses+POS machine-authored.

## Proposed fix

Source glosses from licensed data (Wiktextract/kaikki full coverage, FreeDict, or a licensed bilingual dictionary); keep MT only with explicit approval and disclosure.

## Acceptance criteria

No glosses depend on an unofficial translation endpoint; sources/licences recorded.

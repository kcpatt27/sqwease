## Summary

When romaji word count exceeds JA segment count, trailing segments are empty but still wrapped in colored spans.

## Evidence

`colorizeByWords('水','mizu (mee-zoo)','water',false)` yields jaHtml ending in `<span style="color:..."></span>`.

## Proposed fix

Skip empty segments before wrapping; prefer the token path. Root cause of the extra fake word is T-004.

## Acceptance criteria

No empty `<span>` appears in rendered output for any fixture, including parenthetical pronunciations.

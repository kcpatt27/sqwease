## Summary

FSRS personalization comes from fitting its ~21 weights to the learner's own review history. That needs a review log and enough data; defer until then.

## Evidence

ts-fsrs optimizer is a separate WASI package (@open-spaced-repetition/binding); the fsrs4anki optimizer uses ML over review history.

## Proposed fix

Persist a review log (part of T-096); later, run the optimizer in-browser (WASI) or offline, with default weights as the fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

Optimizer produces sane weights from a sufficiently large log and is strictly optional.

## Summary

Inline `onclick`/`oninput` attributes are used throughout, blocking a strict CSP and complicating keyboard support.

## Evidence

Many `onclick="..."` attributes across pages.

## Proposed fix

Move handlers to addEventListener (event delegation for repeated rows). Do this alongside the a11y button conversions (T-032/T-033).

## Acceptance criteria

No inline event attributes remain; behavior unchanged.

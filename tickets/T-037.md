## Summary

Progress/status text updates are not exposed to assistive tech.

## Evidence

Elements updated via textContent with no role="status"/aria-live.

## Proposed fix

Add `role="status" aria-live="polite"` (and aria-atomic where appropriate).

## Acceptance criteria

Changing progress/status is announced without focus movement.

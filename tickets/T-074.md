## Summary

A flag emoji in the h1 renders inconsistently (not supported on Windows Chrome) and is read aloud.

## Evidence

`<h1>🇯🇵 Japanese 1500</h1>`.

## Proposed fix

Drop the emoji or wrap it in aria-hidden and hide it from the accessible name.

## Acceptance criteria

Heading renders consistently; screen reader reads only the text.

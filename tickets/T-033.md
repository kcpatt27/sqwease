## Summary

Primary interactions are clickable divs/spans with no role, focus, or state; keyboard users can only guess at global shortcuts.

## Evidence

`<div class="container" onclick="flipCard()">`, `#practiceCard` JS click, and `<span class="toggle-info" onclick="toggleInfo()">`.

## Proposed fix

Make cards buttons (or role="button" tabindex=0 with Enter/Space) and give the info toggle `aria-expanded`. Announce flip state.

## Acceptance criteria

All card flips and the info toggle work via keyboard with visible focus and correct ARIA states.

## Summary

Primary interaction targets must be real, keyboard-operable controls. The
multi-language UI pass converted the flashcards flip card and the Boxes info
toggle, but the **Boxes practice card is still a mouse-only `<div>`**.

## Evidence

- Done: flashcards card is `role="button" tabindex="0" aria-pressed` with an
  element keydown handler (`flashcards.html:210`); the Boxes info toggle is a
  `<button aria-expanded>` (`boxes.html:394`).
- Remaining: `<div class="practice-card" id="practiceCard">` (`boxes.html:410`)
  has no `role`, `tabindex`, or `aria-pressed`; it is flipped by a click handler
  (`boxes.html:877`) and the global keydown (`boxes.html:899-927`), not by an
  element-level handler.

## Proposed fix

- Mirror the flashcards card on `#practiceCard`: `role="button" tabindex="0"
  aria-pressed`, an element-level Enter/Space handler that calls the flip, and a
  visible focus ring.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Boxes practice card is reachable by Tab, flips with Enter/Space, exposes
  its pressed state, and shows a visible focus ring.
- No duplicate flip from the global keydown when the card is focused.

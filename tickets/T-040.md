## Summary

Card flips and theme transitions ignore the reduced-motion preference.

## Evidence

`transform .6s` / `.5s` flips and global `transition: background-color .3s, color .3s`.

## Proposed fix

Add a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block that collapses transition/animation durations.

## Acceptance criteria

With reduced motion enabled, flips and transitions are effectively instant.

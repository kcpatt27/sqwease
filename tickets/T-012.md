## Summary

SRS writes are unguarded; a QuotaExceededError (or Safari private mode) aborts the rating flow.

## Evidence

`localStorage.setItem('sqwease-flashcards-srs', JSON.stringify(srsCards));` with no try/catch; boxes.html:1011 already guards saveWords.

## Proposed fix

Wrap writes in try/catch, surface a non-fatal warning, and centralize in the shared storage helper (T-060).

## Acceptance criteria

Simulated setItem failure does not throw out of the rating handler; the user sees a warning and the session continues.

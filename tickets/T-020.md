## Summary

The dark palette and all isDark/isDarkTheme plumbing are dead: the two objects are identical.

## Evidence

LIGHT and DARK list the same 15 values; `var palette = isDark ? DARK : LIGHT;` therefore has no effect, yet isDark threads through every coloring call.

## Proposed fix

Either provide genuinely different dark variants (and test contrast, see T-034) or delete DARK/isDark/isDarkTheme and the parameter plumbing.

## Acceptance criteria

No identical-branch code remains; colors verified on both themes.

## Summary

The positional direct/reverse map swaps colors when the number of JA colors and EN content groups differ, and silently clamps out-of-range indices.

## Evidence

For `なぜ日本に行きたいですか。` / "Why do you want to go to Japan?" JA colors are 日本=blue, 行き=red but EN renders Why=blue and Japan=red (swapped). For `いつ起きますか。` all EN content words get one color while the JA time token is uncolored.

## Proposed fix

Drop the positional heuristic or resolve mismatches by token gloss/count first; never clamp silently (log/assert). Add fixtures for question-word and time-tail sentences.

## Acceptance criteria

Documented sample sentences produce JA/EN color pairs that correspond by meaning, verified on all 5 Boxes tabs, list and practice.

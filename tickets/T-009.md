## Summary

Intervals grow unbounded, and `addDays(...).toISOString()` throws once the date exceeds the JS Date range.

## Evidence

Simulating 12 consecutive Easy reviews reaches interval 32,362,587 days; the 13th call throws `RangeError: Invalid time value`. easeFactor grows +0.15 per Easy with no ceiling.

## Proposed fix

Add `MAX_INTERVAL_DAYS` and an ease ceiling; clamp intervals and guard the date conversion (fall back to a max date).

## Acceptance criteria

100 consecutive Easy ratings never throw and keep the due date within a sane bound; a unit test asserts this.

## Summary

A non-numeric stored counter becomes NaN, permanently disabling new-card introduction.

## Evidence

`parseInt(localStorage.getItem('sqwease-flashcards-new-today') || '0')` -> NaN when the stored value is garbage; `NaN < limit` is false forever.

## Proposed fix

Parse with radix 10 and `isFinite` fallback to 0 (matches the safe-storage helper from T-060).

## Acceptance criteria

Garbage counter values recover to 0 and new cards resume.

## Summary

The codebase mixes ES5 `var` and ES2020 `const`/`let`/optional chaining with no transpile step.

## Evidence

index uses const; kana-charts uses var; boxes uses let/const with `?.`.

## Proposed fix

Pick one baseline (modern browsers, const/let) and apply opportunistically — no tooling required.

## Acceptance criteria

No mixed declarations in files that are touched.

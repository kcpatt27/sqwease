## Summary

Three romanization schemes coexist: macrons (kōhī), doubled vowels (koohii), and ad-hoc pronunciation (mah-soo).

## Evidence

Same words romanized three ways across content files.

## Proposed fix

Choose modified Hepburn (kanaToRomaji output) as canonical; regenerate/correct content romaji fields.

## Acceptance criteria

Grep shows one convention; generated romaji matches kanaToRomaji.

## Summary

Best practice is contextual, multimodal input (example sentence + image + audio) rather than isolated word lists. SQWease has sentences and token data; the gaps are images and audio.

## Evidence

Research synthesis + WordCraft (ACM) stress example sentences + audio + images; see research report §5.

## Proposed fix

Start with a small high-frequency image set (licensing recorded), a lazy-loaded image field on the deck contract, and audio from T-097. Expand per language.

## Acceptance criteria

A subset of words show an image and play audio without hurting load performance; licensing is documented in content/.

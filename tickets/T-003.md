## Summary

The builder splits on raw commas (not RFC 4180), caps at 1500 with no warning, and ships truncated definitions/examples for quoted rows.

## Evidence

CSV has 2507 rows; `const MAX = 1500` drops 1007 silently. 39 rows contain commas inside quoted fields; naive split then `parts.slice(3).join(",")` yields stray quotes/garbage.

## Proposed fix

Replace the splitter with a small quote-aware parser (handle `""`), and remove/raise MAX or at minimum `console.warn` the omitted count. Document the intended deck size.

## Acceptance criteria

Every source row parses with correct 4 columns; quoted commas are preserved; the number of skipped rows (if any) is logged.

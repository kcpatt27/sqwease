## Summary

`SyllableColor.colorize`, `SyllableColor.colorizeHtml`, `Romaji.readOneOrTwo`, and the exported particle sets have no callers.

## Evidence

Repo-wide grep finds no callers for these exports; the particle sets are only used internally.

## Proposed fix

Delete unused exports/functions, or wire them in if intended. Keep the public surface equal to actual consumers.

## Acceptance criteria

No exported symbol without a caller remains.

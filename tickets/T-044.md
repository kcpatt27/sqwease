## Summary

Fixed-position controls ignore notches and the viewport meta lacks viewport-fit=cover.

## Evidence

`.theme-toggle { position:fixed; top:20px; right:20px }`; meta viewport has no viewport-fit.

## Proposed fix

Add viewport-fit=cover and use env(safe-area-inset-*) in the fixed offset.

## Acceptance criteria

Controls are not obscured on notched devices.

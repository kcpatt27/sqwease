## Summary

A fixed 400px/320px card height and 5rem word size clip long words and overflow short landscape viewports.

## Evidence

`height: 400px`, overridden to 320px at 329; `.japanese-word { font-size: 5rem }`.

## Proposed fix

Use min-height plus a viewport-relative max-height (for example max-height:70vh) and clamp() font sizing.

## Acceptance criteria

Long words and landscape phones show full content with no clipping or page overflow.

## Summary

Several controls are named only by `title`, which is unreliable for assistive tech.

## Evidence

Selects/buttons/selects rely on title attributes.

## Proposed fix

Add aria-label or visible `<label>` for each.

## Acceptance criteria

Every control has a reliable accessible name.

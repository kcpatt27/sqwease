## Summary

Three independent particle/helper tables disagree, so the same sentence colors differently on Flashcards vs Boxes.

## Evidence

syllable-color PARTICLES includes 'te','ta','node','kedo' (absent from Romaji); Romaji has 'e','nado','shika','dake' (absent from syllable-color); 'want' is uncolored on Flashcards, colored on Boxes; question words are helpers on Flashcards, colored on Boxes.

## Proposed fix

Delete the duplicate lists from syllable-color.js; consume `Romaji.isParticleToken` and one shared EN-helper set. Let BoxesColoring apply only explicit, documented overrides.

## Acceptance criteria

For a shared fixture list of sentences, `shouldColor` is identical between the flashcards and boxes paths except where a documented Boxes override applies.

## Summary

When a target language has no Boxes/Kana/Kanji/Editor, the menu card is visually
disabled (`pointer-events:none`, `aria-disabled="true"`, `tabindex="-1"`) but
still carries an `href`. A screen-reader user or programmatic activation can
still follow the link to a page that is not available for that language.

## Evidence

- `.menu a.is-disabled { pointer-events: none; }` (index.html:91).
- `syncCapabilities()` sets `aria-disabled` and removes tabindex but never the
  `href` (index.html ~208-220).

## Proposed fix

- When disabled, remove `href` (and keep the note), or intercept activation and
  announce "not available for <language>". Restore the `href` when re-enabled.

## Acceptance criteria

- Disabled cards cannot navigate by mouse, keyboard, or assistive tech.
- Enabling the language restores the link.

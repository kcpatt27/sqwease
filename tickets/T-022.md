## Summary

`escapeHtml` is reimplemented 5x, `normalize` 2x, the kanji regex inline 2x, and EN_HELPERS 2x with drifting contents.

## Evidence

Same DOM-trick escape each time; EN_HELPERS differs (Boxes omits `want`, colors question words).

## Proposed fix

Promote escapeHtml/normalize/containsKanji/isParticleToken to one shared util; have colorizeByTokens actually call Romaji.shouldColorToken instead of reimplementing it (contradicts its own docstring).

## Acceptance criteria

One definition of each helper; grep finds no duplicates.

## Summary

A Latin-first stack (Segoe UI/Tahoma/...) lets Japanese glyphs fall back to an arbitrary serif.

## Evidence

`font-family: 'Segoe UI', Tahoma, Geneva, Verdana, sans-serif;` on every page.

## Proposed fix

Append a CJK stack (Noto Sans JP, Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN, Yu Gothic, Meiryo) and/or use `:lang(ja)`.

## Acceptance criteria

Japanese renders in a consistent sans-serif across Windows/macOS/mobile.

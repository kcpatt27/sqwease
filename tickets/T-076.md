## Summary

The doc claims sentence romaji equals space-joined token romaji and that EN is never reversed, both false for shipped data/code.

## Evidence

Punctuation token and split verbs break the equality; boxes-coloring does reverse EN groups.

## Proposed fix

Correct the invariants (exclude punctuation, note verb-stem splitting) and add a build-time validator.

## Acceptance criteria

Documented invariants hold or are explicitly qualified; validator catches violations.

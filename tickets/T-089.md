## Summary

Token colors are computed once from `isDarkTheme()` at render time. Only `editor.html` listens for `sqwease:themechange` and re-renders; flashcards and boxes keep the old-palette colors until the card is re-rendered.

## Evidence

editor.html has `window.addEventListener('sqwease:themechange', renderCurrent)`; flashcards/boxes have no such listener while their palettes are theme-dependent.

## Proposed fix

Add the same `sqwease:themechange` → re-render listener to flashcards and boxes.

## Acceptance criteria

Toggling dark mode immediately recolors the visible card/token spans on flashcards and boxes.

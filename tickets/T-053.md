## Summary

Several interactive controls are below the 44px minimum outside the mobile media query.

## Evidence

.wordlist-cat padding 8x12, .remove-btn 4-6px, .result-btn 6x12.

## Proposed fix

Apply min-height/min-width 44px to interactive controls globally.

## Acceptance criteria

All tap targets meet 44x44 CSS px at all breakpoints.

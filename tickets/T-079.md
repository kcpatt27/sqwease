## Summary

At phone widths the 1-2-3 Boxes page is wider than the viewport, producing a
horizontal scrollbar. The `.mode-tabs` row (three buttons plus the display-phase
select) is a non-wrapping flex row whose min-content width is wider than the
screen.

## Evidence

Measured via a same-origin iframe at 320 px wide:

- `documentElement.scrollWidth` = 368 vs `clientWidth` = 305.
- The widest chain is `MAIN` → `.container` → `.mode-tabs` at 431 px
  (min-content). `.set-tabs` wraps (line 108) but `.mode-tabs` has no
  `flex-wrap`.

## Proposed fix

- Add `flex-wrap: wrap` to `.mode-tabs` and let the display-phase select wrap to
  its own line on narrow screens.
- Re-check the Word lists tab at 320 px.

## Acceptance criteria

- No horizontal overflow on `boxes.html` at 320/375 px in all three modes
  (list, practice, word lists).
- Nothing is clipped; controls remain ≥44 px.

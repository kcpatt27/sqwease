## Summary

Physical left/right properties and text-align:left assume LTR.

## Evidence

Fixed side margins/paddings and text-align:left.

## Proposed fix

Prefer margin-inline/padding-inline and text-align:start where the visual result is unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

Pages render equivalently LTR and do not break if dir=rtl is set.

## Summary

Meaning is conveyed by hue alone, and several palette colors are far below 4.5:1 on light backgrounds.

## Evidence

s_YELLOW `hsl(59,100%,45%)` is about 1.4:1 on white; no redundant cue (underline/weight/pattern) distinguishes aligned colors.

## Proposed fix

Darken light palette entries to >=4.5:1 on both themes and add a non-color alignment cue; verify per-theme (ties to T-020).

## Acceptance criteria

All palette colors pass 4.5:1 for text on their theme background; color is not the sole carrier of the JA<->romaji<->EN mapping.
